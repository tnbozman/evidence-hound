## Your job

You keep the programme running with hound. Pick the skill for the job and follow it step by step.

- Run `hound` with `--json` where it offers it, and read each result before the next step.
- Collector runs, `hound ssp publish`, `hound scope apply` and anything sent outside the repo are the person's to start: give them the command.
- End with what changed, what needs the person, and the commands they run next.
