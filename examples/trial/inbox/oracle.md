Fictional notes for the Evidence Hound trial, written as Example Pty Ltd's GRC team would keep them. They are not Oracle's documents
and make no claim about Oracle's real certifications: in a real review, keep the supplier's own reports under `documents/suppliers/`.

- **What we buy:** OCI Compute, networking and block storage, pay as you go (contract CTR-OCI-2026).
- **What depends on it:** GRC-PLATFORM, the one private VM that runs CISO Assistant and Evidence Hound. No public IP address; the office
  reaches it over a site-to-site VPN. Moving it elsewhere is a planned project, not a quick swap.
- **Access:** Oracle hosts the VM and its disks. It holds no account in our identity provider and does not administer the VM.
- **Assurance seen:** none kept yet. The account owner says the provider's trust centre lists independent audit reports; ask the
  person before treating that as evidence.
