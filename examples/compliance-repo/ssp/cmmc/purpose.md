Example CUI environment is the part of Example Pty Ltd's IT that stores, processes or sends Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) for its defence contracts: mail, files and identity in Example 365, the staff laptops, and the backup service that protects both. The contracts carry DFARS 252.204-7012, so the company implements NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 and is assessed at CMMC Level 2.
