# Runbook: yearly MFA exceptions review

CISO Assistant emails this to the IT team before the yearly due date.

## What to export

1. In the Example 365 admin centre, export the list of accounts excluded from the MFA policy as CSV.
2. Beside each account, write why it is excluded (such as a break-glass account kept in the safe) and who approved it.
3. Remove any exclusion nobody can explain, then export the list again.

## Send it to the GRC team

Attach the final CSV with its reasons to this year's task in CISO Assistant, or email it to the GRC team, who attach
it. The GRC team reviews it and sets the requirement's result.
