- **Severity 1:** a system that handles customer or controlled information is compromised, or the business cannot trade. Example
  Pty Ltd's incident manager starts the response at once, day or night.
- **Severity 2:** one system or account is compromised and contained to it, such as a laptop with malware or a phished mailbox.
  The response starts the same business day.
- **Severity 3:** a suspicious event with no sign of harm, such as a blocked phishing email reported by staff. Logged and reviewed
  in the weekly review.
