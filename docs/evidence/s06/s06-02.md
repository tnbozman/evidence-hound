# s06-02 Essential Eight 2023 ML2 mapping for the checks we have

**Status:** Done (2026-11-21).

## What was done
- `mappings/essential-eight-2023.yaml` targets `urn:intuitem:risk:framework:essential-eight-2023` (never the legacy `essential-eight`). It maps the M365 collector's checks and the example manual sources to 16 ML1 and ML2 requirements: ISM-1504, ISM-1173, ISM-0445, ISM-1883, ISM-1501, ISM-1695, ISM-1488, ISM-1673, ISM-1667, ISM-1668, ISM-1669, ISM-0123, ISM-0140, ISM-1819, ISM-1515 and ISM-1582. ISM controls repeated across strategies (ISM-0123, ISM-0140 and ISM-1819) use `rows: all`.
- The other 71 distinct ML1 and ML2 refs are listed under the new top-level `manual_only` key, each with a suggested manual source. `manual_only` is a coverage record: it is loaded, schema-checked and validated against the snapshot (`mapping.urn_unknown`, `mapping.ref_unknown`, `mapping.ref_ambiguous`), but never published. The JSON Schema adds `$defs.manualOnly`.
- `examples/example-essential-eight.yaml`: an M365 collector with fictional IDs, the manual sources `example-incident-register`, `example-backup-restore-test` and `example-application-control-review`, and the target `EX-E8-2026` (the seed's Essential Eight 2023 audit, maturity levels 1 and 2). ML3-only rows are outside the audit's groups, so they are skipped rather than reported as errors (s06-01).
- The `essential-eight-2023` snapshot was committed in s06-01.
