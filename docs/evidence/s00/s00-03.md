## What was done
- `ci.yml` runs on push to `main` and `sprint/**` and on PRs to `main`: restore, format check, build, unit tests with TRX and Cobertura coverage, results uploaded. Steps call the Makefile targets.
- `org-data` job runs `scripts/check-org-data.sh` with the `ORG_DENYLIST` secret; `secrets` job runs gitleaks; `dependency-review` fails on high severity.

- `scripts/tests/check-org-data.bats` (5 tests) proves a term in a file or commit message fails the check, case-insensitively, without printing the term, and an empty deny-list exits 2.
