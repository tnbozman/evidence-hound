  A source can be marked as needing a licence. When Graph answers it with a licence error (`m365.graph_not_licensed`), the run goes on without it; any other failure, or a licence error on a required collection, still fails the run.
- Checks (`PrivilegedChecks`), over the roles in `privileged_roles` (default: 14 built-in administrator roles):
  - `identity.privileged.global_admin_count`: accounts holding Global Administrator, active or eligible, between `global_admin_min` and `global_admin_max` (default 2 to 4, break-glass accounts included). The observation splits active and eligible-only holders. Config with min above max is invalid.
  - `identity.privileged.admins_cloud_only`: user accounts holding a privileged role (active or eligible) are not synchronised from on-premises; synchronised ones are named by object ID. Groups or apps holding roles are counted and noted, not assessed.
  - `identity.privileged.pim_eligible_not_permanent`: no privileged role is permanently assigned (`Assigned` with no end date) except to the documented break-glass accounts. Time-bound assignments and activations are fine. `not_applicable` when the tenant has no PIM.
- The recorded tenant gains two permanent break-glass Global Administrators, two admins eligible for Global Administrator and other roles, one activated Exchange Administrator assignment, and a permanent non-privileged Directory Readers assignment.
- The CLI composition root gains an internal overload taking both extra settings and extra services, so the functional test can give the real `hound run` a test credential.
