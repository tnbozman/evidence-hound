# Operating guides

Short guides for each person who keeps the programme running. They assume the setup in the
[first deployment](../tutorials/first-deployment.md) is done.

| Guide | For | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [GRC member: your week, month and quarter](grc-member.md) | The one person who runs compliance, 2 to 8 hours a week | 2 to 8 hours a week |
| [System owner](system-owner.md) | People who own a system and provide its manual evidence | Minutes a month |
| [Collector operator](collector-operator.md) | Whoever runs hound's container: IT, often the GRC member | An hour a month |
| [Committee member](committee-member.md) | Members of the monthly security committee | 30 minutes a month |

Training outlines that go with them are in [`docs/training/`](../training/README.md).
