# Training: the monthly committee (30 minutes)

For security committee members. Built on [reading the committee report](../operate/committee-member.md) and
[the security committee guide](../govern/security-committee.md).

**By the end, each person can:** read a committee report in 15 minutes, decide on proposed actions and expiring exceptions, and say
how each decision reaches CISO Assistant.

**Before the session:** the presenter takes two example snapshots and generates the report from the local stack
(`hound report snapshot --period <last month>` and the month before, then `hound report committee`). Hand out the printed report.

| Minutes | Topic | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 5 | What the committee is for | The terms of reference in two sentences; the committee's authority versus the board's. |
| 5 to 15 | Reading the report | Summary, then actions, exceptions and overdue evidence, in the order of the reading guide. Words members will see. |
| 15 to 25 | Exercise | In pairs: decide each proposed action (agree, change or drop), and one expiring exception (renew or fix). Each pair says who owns it and by when. |
| 25 to 30 | Closing the loop | The presenter records one decision in CISO Assistant and shows where next month's report will show it. |

**Check understanding:**

1. A risk acceptance expires in 45 days. Where is it in the report, and what are the options? (Section 4, expiring within 60 days;
   renew by a new decision, or fix the gap.)
2. An owner is overdue for a second month. What does the committee do? (Asks them why, and confirms an action with a date in section 8.)
3. Someone wants to accept a risk for two years. Can the committee approve it? (No: longer than 12 months needs the board.)
