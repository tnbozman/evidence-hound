# Training: the quarterly board report (20 minutes)

For board members. Built on [reporting to the board](../govern/board-reporting.md).

**By the end, each person can:** read the board report's compliance positions and trends, understand the risks accepted on the board's
behalf, and know what the board is asked to decide.

**Before the session:** on the local stack, the presenter takes a snapshot for the last month of this quarter and of the three quarters
before (`hound report snapshot --period YYYY-MM` for each; they all read today's data, which is enough for training), then generates
`hound report board` and prints it.

| Minutes | Topic | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 5 | What the board sets | The risk appetite statement, and what the committee may decide without the board. |
| 5 to 12 | Reading the report | Compliance position per programme and the fixed rules behind on track, on track with conditions and off track; the four-quarter trend; key risks against appetite. |
| 12 to 17 | Exercise | Decide on one acceptance marked for board endorsement: endorse, shorten or decline, and say what that means for its owner. |
| 17 to 20 | Recording decisions | How the decision is recorded in CISO Assistant and appears next quarter. |

**Check understanding:**

1. Why is a programme off track although most requirements are compliant? (A requirement got worse since last quarter, or more than a
   quarter are non-compliant.)
2. What marks an acceptance for endorsement? (Valid for more than 12 months, or no expiry.)
3. Who proposes the compliance position, and who confirms it? (Management proposes it by fixed rules; the authorising official
   confirms it; the board notes it.)
